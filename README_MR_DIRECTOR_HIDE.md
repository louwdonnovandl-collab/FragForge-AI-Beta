# FragForge AI v0.10.28 — Mr Director Peek & Hide
Mr Director now uses a more playful big-headed vinyl-toy/Funko-inspired illustration.

Click his full character to hear: "Okay, I'll hide! Click my head when you need me."
After one second, the full character and speech bubble disappear; a half-head peeking button remains at the edge of the AI Director dialog.
Click the peeking head to restore the character and speech bubble.
The chat and plan controls remain available while he's hiding.

No paid API; Ollama integration and existing Auto Edit remain unchanged.
Note: The character is a static illustration and the hide/reveal uses a short delay, not animated movement.
