# Editor-X: 20-slot NCS user-import collection and montage audio cleanup

- NCS songs are NOT included in this ZIP. NCS terms permit independent creator UGC with attribution, but not redistribution of audio in the FragForge installer without permission. Users can import up to 20 tracks they downloaded themselves from NCS.
- Open Prepare Edit > Edit Details > Import NCS song. Enter the exact song and artist for attribution. Personal copies are stored under ~/Music/FragForge/NCS and are automatically scanned at startup.
- Ask Editor-X for the song by title, or say 'NCS music' to randomize among imported tracks. Imported track credits appear in the applied edit summary.
- Selected background music is placed on A1; short transition SFX remain on A2.
- AI montage gameplay volume reduced to 30%, music to 42%. Manually edited timeline clips are unchanged.
- Removed repeated black-flash video fades on every interior cut; initial and final fades remain. This is NOT a true video crossfade. A proper overlapping video transition still requires a separate render-pipeline change.
- Current music repeats/shortens to montage duration. Full NCS tracks can be shortened for timing, not remixed or altered in fundamental character.
- Music and edit changes are applied only after timeline confirmation. Export on Windows still needs listening and visual QA.
